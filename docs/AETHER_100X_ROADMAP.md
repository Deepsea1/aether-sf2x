# Aether Measurement Roadmap

"10x" and "100x" are measurement programs, not performance promises.

## Ordered phases

1. Freeze baseline, audit every consumer, and record capability truth.
2. Add canonical truth vocabulary and contracts; fast paths stay model-assessed
   unless actual evidence earns more.
3. Add atomic claims and the deterministic Truth Gate in shadow mode.
4. Harden secure retrieval, exact source identity, origin graph, and evidence
   applicability.
5. Add deterministic computation services and traces.
6. Add typed memory, corrections, invalidation, cache/warrant revocation.
7. Add outcome adjudication and holdout-gated calibration.
8. Add independent reproduction, public limitations, operational continuity,
   and external challenge processes.

## Non-promotion gates

Do not increase automated authority while high-impact false verification exceeds
the approved threshold, citation entailment or applicability is unmeasured,
corrections cannot propagate, required outages can yield a factual-green result,
tenant isolation is unproven, or benchmark integrity is contaminated.

## Measurement discipline

Publish sample size, confidence interval, exclusions, severity, cost, latency,
and methodology. Never turn model self-reporting into an operational metric.
