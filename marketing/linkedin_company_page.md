## Description
Aether by SF2X is an AI trust verification layer that catches LLM hallucinations in real time.

Using a 3-model tribunal architecture — Proposer, Critic, and Verifier — Aether cross-examines AI responses to catch fabricated citations, false premises, and adversarial outputs that single-model guardrails miss. Every answer gets a cryptographic warrant with premises, sources, and a signed hash, making it audit-ready.

Benchmark results: Plain LLMs score 14/100 trustworthiness (86% hallucination rate). Aether's tribunal scores 91/100 — certified, with AUC 1.0 and Pearson r=0.98, achieving perfect separation between true and hallucinated claims.
